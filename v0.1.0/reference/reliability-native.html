Native single-rating intraclass correlation#

reliability::icc(&NumericBuffer, IccType, confidence) accepts a complete, balanced subject-by-reader matrix: one subject per row, one reader per column. At least two subjects and two readers are required. Missing values, invalid buffer dimensions and invalid confidence are explicit errors; there is no automatic omission or imputation. The current Python adapter accepts the matrix, not subject/reader ID columns. The caller must first align the complete data by explicit subject and reader IDs and verify unique subject-reader pairs. Matrix shape validation alone cannot prove identity or pairing.

Choose the type explicitly:

  • IccType::Icc21, serialized as ICC(2,1): two-way random-effects absolute agreement, single rating.

  • IccType::Icc31, serialized as ICC(3,1): two-way fixed-reader consistency, single rating.

Both use the same balanced two-way ANOVA mean squares. The random-reader estimate includes the reader mean-square adjustment; the consistency estimate removes that component. Confidence limits use the Shrout–Fleiss ANOVA/F construction frozen from public Pingouin 0.5.5, with presentation rounding disabled. All F probabilities and quantiles call the shared native statistics::Distribution implementation. There is no separate F CDF here.

The result reports its type, subject/reader counts, confidence, estimate and unrounded limits, F statistic, numerator/denominator degrees of freedom, p-value and all three ANOVA mean squares. Negative estimates and confidence bounds are retained. No value is clipped to zero to satisfy a reliability threshold.

Degenerate inputs retain the reference’s numerical state. Completely constant ratings produce NaN for the estimate, F, p-value and confidence limits. Perfect agreement with varying subjects has estimate one, infinite F, p-value zero and undefined confidence limits in the frozen reference. Results contain explicit quality diagnostics and serialize NaN/Inf using the shared scalar/vector serializers; undefined components are not replaced by zero or null.

The independently frozen fixture checks balanced data, reader bias, low agreement with negative estimates, perfect agreement, constant ratings and missing-value failure for both types. Estimates, F, p-values and unrounded confidence limits pass atol=1e-10, rtol=1e-8; nonfinite states match by position. These results do not implement average-measure ICC, missing/unbalanced mixed models, generalized reader designs or an unspecified ICC threshold interface.

Definition and reference provenance: Pingouin 0.5.5 public reliability source, golden/python_golden_v1/icc_reference.json and its pinned dependency lock.
